PROLOGUE

The Owl Who Stopped Time

Falls Creek Village · dawn, the second morning

When Chloe and Leo woke, something was wrong. Every clock in the Village had stopped — all frozen at exactly 7 o'clock. The chairlifts stood still. Even the snowflakes seemed to hang in the air.

A great white owl swooped down onto the frozen clock tower. "Time has stopped," it hooted softly, "because someone forgot how to count it. Only clever explorers who can read the clock, count in steps, and know their number magic can wake the mountain. Will you help?"

Leo puffed out his chest. "We found the Golden Snowflake. We can do anything!"

The Owl blinked its huge golden eyes. "Then let us begin — before winter freezes forever."

EPISODE 1Telling the Time

The Frozen Clocks

The Village clock tower
Previously: Every clock has frozen at 7 o'clock. The Snow Owl needs help waking time itself…

"To start a clock," said the Owl, "you must read it correctly. The big hand shows the minutes; the little hand shows the hour." Chloe nodded — she'd practised this. The first clock's hands began to glow, waiting for an answer.

🔓 DECODE THIS! — Reading the Clocks
Each correct time restarts one frozen clock.
  1. The little hand points to 3, the big hand points straight up to 12. What time is it?
  2. The clock shows half past 4 (big hand on 6). Write it as _:_ .
  3. It is 9 o'clock now. What time will it be in 2 hours?
  4. The ski school starts at 10:00 and lasts 1 hour. What time does it finish?
  5. It is quarter past 2 (big hand on the 3). Write the time.
Solutions: 1) 3:00   2) 4:30   3) 11:00   4) 11:00   5) 2:15

One by one the clocks ticked back to life — tick, tick, tick! "Wonderful," hooted the Owl. "But the chairlifts still sleep. They only wake to a special counting song."

EPISODE 2Skip Counting (2s · 5s · 10s)

The Counting Song of the Chairlifts

The Summit chairlift base
Previously: The clocks are ticking again — but the chairlifts wake only to a skip-counting song…

"Skip counting," explained the Owl, "is counting in jumps instead of ones. Count by 2s: 2, 4, 6, 8… Count by 5s: 5, 10, 15… Count by 10s: 10, 20, 30…" Each chair on the lift had a number, and they had to sing the pattern to make it move.

🔓 DECODE THIS! — The Counting Song
Fill each blank to send the chairlift climbing.
  1. Count by 2s: 2, 4, 6, 8, ___ ?
  2. Count by 5s: 5, 10, 15, 20, ___ ?
  3. Count by 10s: 10, 20, 30, ___ ?
  4. There are 6 chairs. Each seats 2 people. Count by 2s — how many people in all?
  5. Count by 5s to find how many fingers on 4 hands: 5, 10, 15, ___ ?
Solutions: 1) 10   2) 25   3) 40   4) 12 (2,4,6,8,10,12)   5) 20

With a great whirr, the chairlifts swung into motion, carrying empty seats up into the pink dawn. The Owl smiled. "Two puzzles solved. But now — the trickiest magic of all. Odd and even."

EPISODE 3Odd & Even Numbers

The Odd-and-Even Bridge

A snow bridge over Rocky Valley Creek
Previously: Clocks tick, chairlifts climb — now an icy bridge blocks the way, split into odd and even stones…

A narrow snow bridge crossed the creek, each stepping-stone carved with a number. "Step only on the EVEN stones," warned the Owl. "Even numbers can be shared into two equal groups — 2, 4, 6, 8. Odd numbers always have one left over — 1, 3, 5, 7. Step on an odd stone and — splash!"

🔓 DECODE THIS! — The Safe Stones
Choose the even numbers to cross safely.
  1. Which of these are EVEN?   3, 4, 7, 8, 10
  2. Is 6 odd or even?
  3. Is 9 odd or even?
  4. If Leo has 8 marbles, can he share them equally between 2 people with none left over?
  5. What is the next even number after 12?
Solutions: 1) 4, 8, 10 are even   2) even   3) odd   4) yes (4 each)   5) 14

Chloe and Leo hopped stone to stone — 4, 8, 10 — and reached the far side, dry and cheering. Waiting for them was a locked ice-gate, humming with numbers. "Ah," said the Owl gravely. "The Gate of Ten. This one tests everything so far."

The gate dissolved into glittering snow. Beyond it, a spiral staircase of ice wound up into the clouds toward the Owl's Nest. "Almost there," said the Owl. "But the final magic is the deepest of all — the magic of Number Bonds."

EPISODE 4Number Bonds to 10 & 20

The Bonds That Make Ten

The spiral ice staircase
Previously: The Gate of Ten is open; a spiral staircase rises toward the Owl's Nest…

"Number bonds," said the Owl, "are pairs of numbers that snap together to make a whole. 6 and 4 make 10. 7 and 3 make 10. Knowing them makes you count like lightning!" Each ice step showed one number of a pair — say the partner, and the step turned solid.

🔓 DECODE THIS! — Bonds to Ten (and Twenty)
Name the partner that completes each bond.
  1. 6 + ___ = 10
  2. 3 + ___ = 10
  3. 8 + ___ = 10
  4. 15 + ___ = 20
  5. Leo has 7 snowballs. How many more to make 10?
Solutions: 1) 4   2) 7   3) 2   4) 5   5) 3

Step by glowing step, Chloe and Leo climbed. At the very top waited the Owl's Nest — and inside it, one last, enormous test, its numbers spinning like a blizzard.

EPISODE 5Time Word Problems

Riddles in the Snow

The doorway of the Owl's Nest
Previously: At the top of the ice stairs waits the Owl's Nest and one final challenge…

"Before the last test," hooted the Owl, "one set of story-riddles — the kind where time hides inside a tale. Read like a detective!"

🔓 DECODE THIS! — Time Story-Riddles
  1. Chloe starts skiing at 10:00 and skis for 2 hours. What time does she stop?
  2. The gondola leaves every 5 minutes. If one leaves at 9:00, when does the next one go?
  3. Lunch is at 12:30. It is 11:30 now. How many hours until lunch?
  4. Leo counts 5 chairlifts pass every minute. How many pass in 2 minutes?
  5. The fireworks start at 8:00 and last 15 minutes. What time do they finish?
Solutions: 1) 12:00   2) 9:05   3) 1 hour   4) 10   5) 8:15

The Owl spread its great wings. "You are ready. Enter the Nest — and face the Clock of the Snow Owl itself."

🏆 GRAND FINALE

The Clock of the Snow Owl

Inside the Owl's Nest · mixes all of Book Two
Previously: Everything today led here — the great Clock of the Snow Owl, with fifteen numbers to set right…

A magnificent clock filled the Nest, its face ringed with fifteen empty spaces. "Fill them all," said the Owl, "and time will flow free forever."

🏆 THE FIFTEEN-NUMBER CLOCK — the final challenge!
Everything from Book Two, easy → hard. Take 30 minutes!
  1. What time is half past 3? (_:_)
  2. It is 7:00. What time in 4 hours?
  3. Count by 2s: 14, 16, 18, ___ ?
  4. Count by 5s: 25, 30, 35, ___ ?
  5. Count by 10s: 60, 70, ___ ?
  6. Is 21 odd or even?
  7. Which is even: 9 or 14?
  8. 4 + ___ = 10
  9. 13 + ___ = 20
  10. Quarter past 6 — write the time.
  11. The bus leaves at 9:15 and the trip takes 30 minutes. What time does it arrive?
  12. Can 14 cookies be shared equally between 2 people? How many each?
  13. Count by 2s to find the people on 7 two-seater chairs: how many?
  14. Leo has 9 stickers. How many more to make 20?
  15. It is 11:00. Lunch was 2 hours ago. What time was lunch?
Clock code: 1) 3:30   2) 11:00   3) 20   4) 40   5) 80   6) odd   7) 14   8) 6   9) 7   10) 6:15   11) 9:45   12) yes, 7 each   13) 14   14) 11   15) 9:00
Final rank: 13–15 = 🏆 Keeper of Time! · 9–12 = 🥈 Brilliant! · 5–8 = 🥉 A true explorer · under 5 = climb the stairs again, you'll get it!

As the last number clicked into place, the great Clock chimed — BONG! — and all across Falls Creek, time rushed joyfully back to life. Snow fell, lifts hummed, children laughed. The Snow Owl bowed its head. "You have given the mountain its time again. Well done, keepers." Then it spread its wings and vanished into the clouds — leaving one small feather, and a note: "Tomorrow, learn to share…"
