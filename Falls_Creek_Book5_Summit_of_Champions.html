EPISODE 1Angles & Right Angles

The Corner of Right Angles

A cliff of perfectly square ice blocks
Previously: The Snow Fox meets Chloe and Leo at the final peak for the greatest challenge…

"An angle is the amount of turn at a corner," said the Fox. "A right angle is a perfect square corner — like the corner of a book or a window. It looks like an L. More open than that is 'bigger than a right angle'; more closed is 'smaller'."

🔓 DECODE THIS! — Right Angles
  1. How many right angles (square corners) does a square have?
  2. Does the corner of a book make a right angle — yes or no?
  3. A clock's hands at 3 o'clock make what kind of angle — a right angle, or bigger?
  4. How many right angles does a rectangle have?
  5. Is the pointy tip of a slice of pizza smaller or bigger than a right angle?
Solutions: 1) 4   2) yes   3) a right angle   4) 4   5) smaller

The square ice blocks locked into a stairway. "Well turned!" said the Fox. "Now — can you find your WAY? A champion never gets lost."

EPISODE 2Direction & Grid Position

The Compass and the Grid

A snowfield marked like a giant chessboard
Previously: Right angles conquered; now a giant grid tests whether the explorers can find their way…

"Directions," said the Fox, "are North, South, East, West. And on a grid, we find a spot by counting across first, then up." A huge grid stretched over the snow, with a little flag on it.

up 3 . . 🦊 . 2 . ⛄ . . 1 . . . 🎁 0 1 2 3 4 across
🔓 DECODE THIS! — Compass & Grid
  1. If you face North and turn right, which direction do you face now?
  2. On the grid, the gift 🎁 is at "across 4, up 1". What is at "across 2, up 2"? (Look!)
  3. Which is opposite to East?
  4. The snowman ⛄ is at across 2, up 2. Move it 1 square East. What is its new "across" number?
  5. If you walk North then turn around (about-face), which way do you face?
Solutions: 1) East   2) the snowman ⛄   3) West   4) across 3   5) South

The grid glowed and pointed the way onward. "Now for the trickiest idea of all," said the Fox with a sly smile. "CHANCE. What is likely… and what is not?"

EPISODE 3Chance & Probability

The Game of Chance

A frozen lake where the Fox plays games
Previously: The grid pointed onward; now the Snow Fox tests what is likely, unlikely, and certain…

"Some things are certain (sure to happen), some impossible (can never happen), and some are just likely or unlikely," said the Fox, pulling out a bag of marbles.

🔓 DECODE THIS! — Likely or Unlikely?
  1. A bag has 9 red marbles and 1 blue. If you grab one without looking, which colour are you MORE likely to get?
  2. Is it certain, likely, or impossible that snow will fall UP into the sky?
  3. Is it certain or impossible that tomorrow comes after today?
  4. A bag has only white marbles. Pick one — what colour is it CERTAIN to be?
  5. A bag has 5 red and 5 green. Are you equally likely to pick red or green — yes or no?
Solutions: 1) red   2) impossible   3) certain   4) white   5) yes

The Fox clapped. "Only true champions understand chance! But before the summit — one great test of everything on the way up." Ahead loomed an archway sealed with ten glowing runes.

A rune-sealed arch near the peak · mixes Episodes 1–3

EPISODE 4Roman Numerals (fun!)

The Ancient Numbers

A ring of ancient carved stones
Previously: The Archway opened; now ancient stones teach the numbers of long ago…

"Long ago," said the Fox, "people wrote numbers with letters. I = 1, V = 5, X = 10. Put them together to count: II = 2, III = 3, VI = 6 (5 and 1 more)." Carved stones waited to be read.

🔓 DECODE THIS! — Ancient Numbers
  1. What number is the Roman numeral III?
  2. What number is V?
  3. What number is X?
  4. What number is VII (5 and 2 more)?
  5. Write the number 2 in Roman numerals.
Solutions: 1) 3   2) 5   3) 10   4) 7   5) II

The stones lit up in a ring, and a final stairway of light rose to the very peak of Falls Creek. At the top stood a magnificent golden trophy, ringed with fifteen glowing locks. "The Champion's Cup," breathed the Fox. "The last test of your whole journey. Everything you have ever learned, brave ones. Go — become Champions."

The very Summit of Falls Creek · mixes ALL FIVE BOOKS — a real AMC-style test

As the fifteenth lock clicked home, the Champion's Cup blazed like a second sun. Fireworks of ice burst over the whole mountain, and all the friends from the journey appeared — the Snow Owl, the fair Wombat, the mighty Dragon — cheering together. The Snow Fox placed a small golden medal in each child's hand.

"You counted, you measured, you shared, you thought, and you NEVER gave up. That is what makes a true Champion of the Snow. Wherever you go, the mountain will remember Chloe and Leo. — The Snow Fox 🦊🏆"

And as the sun set gold and pink over Falls Creek, Chloe and Leo stood at the very top of the world, medals gleaming, ready for whatever adventures — and whatever maths — might come next winter.
