## Text 1 — Narrative 故事类

**The Lost Kitten**

Ruby was walking home from school when she heard a tiny sound coming from behind the bin. "Meow... meow..."

She peeked behind the bin and saw a small grey kitten. It was shivering and its fur was wet from the rain. The kitten looked up at Ruby with big green eyes.

"You poor thing," Ruby whispered. She took off her jumper and gently wrapped the kitten inside it.

Ruby carried the kitten home. Her mum helped her dry its fur with a warm towel and gave it a saucer of milk. The kitten drank it all and then curled up on Ruby's lap, purring softly.

"Can we keep him, Mum? Please?" Ruby asked.

Mum smiled. "First we must put up posters to see if he belongs to someone. If nobody comes, then he can stay."

Ruby crossed her fingers behind her back. She had already thought of the perfect name: Storm.

## Text 2 — Information Report 说明文

**Wombats**

*What is a wombat?*
Wombats are Australian animals with short legs, strong claws and thick brown fur. An adult wombat can weigh up to 35 kilograms — about the same as a ten-year-old child!

*Where do wombats live?*
Wombats live in burrows, which are long tunnels they dig under the ground. A wombat's burrow can be up to 30 metres long. Wombats sleep in their burrows during the day and come out at night to eat. Animals that are active at night are called **nocturnal**.

*What do wombats eat?*
Wombats eat grasses, roots and bark. Their front teeth never stop growing, so all that chewing keeps them the right length.

*An amazing fact*
Wombats are the only animals in the world that do cube-shaped poo! Scientists believe the cube shape stops it from rolling away, which helps wombats mark their territory.

## Text 3 — Procedure 步骤类

**How to Make a Paper Cup Telephone**

*You will need:*
- 2 paper cups
- a long piece of string (about 5 metres)
- a sharp pencil
- a friend

*Steps:*
1. Use the pencil to poke a small hole in the bottom of each cup.
2. Push one end of the string through the hole in the first cup. Tie a big knot inside the cup so the string cannot slip out.
3. Do the same with the other end of the string and the second cup.
4. Give one cup to your friend and walk apart until the string is pulled tight.
5. Speak into your cup while your friend holds their cup to their ear.

*Handy hint:* The telephone will not work if the string is loose. Make sure it stays tight and does not touch anything.

## Text 4 — Persuasive 议论文

**Kids Need More Playtime!**

Do you think lunchtime at school is long enough? I don't! I believe children should have more playtime at school, and here is why.

Firstly, playing keeps us healthy. Doctors say children should exercise for at least one hour every day. Running, climbing and skipping at playtime are all great exercise.

Secondly, playtime helps our brains. After sitting still and thinking hard in class, our brains get tired. A break outside helps us concentrate better when we return to the classroom. In fact, some schools that added extra playtime found that students' work improved!

Some people say more playtime means less learning. But that's not true — a rested brain learns faster than a tired one.

So come on, principals of Australia — give kids the playtime we need! You will have healthier, happier and smarter students.

### 给家长的批改建议
- 对 20 题以上：非常好，可以进入下一辑。
- 对 15–19 题：把错题按"能力类型"归类（字面/推断/主旨/词义/手法），看是哪类薄弱。
- 对 15 题以下：先降低难度，回到日常朗读+口头问答，不急于刷题。
