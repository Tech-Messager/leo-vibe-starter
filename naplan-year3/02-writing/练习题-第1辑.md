# Writing 练习题 · 第1辑（真题风格写作任务）

> 每次选 1 题，严格 40 分钟（5 构思 + 30 写 + 5 检查）。写完对照《知识点.md》第四节的 10 项标准打分。

---

## Part A — Narrative 记叙文题目（真题风格 prompt）

### 任务 N1：The Box（经典真题风格）

> **The Box**
> One day you find a box. It looks interesting.
> You wonder what could be inside.
>
> Write a narrative (story) about the box.
> Think about:
> - where you find the box and what it looks like
> - what happens when it is opened
> - how your story will end
>
> Remember to plan your story, write in sentences, check your spelling and punctuation.

**构思模板（写之前先填）：**
- Who（谁的故事）：________
- Where & When（在哪、什么时候找到盒子）：________
- 盒子里是什么（越出人意料越好）：________
- Complication（打开后出了什么问题/意外）：________
- Resolution（怎么解决的）：________
- 我的 sizzling start 第一句：________

---

### 任务 N2：The Day the Power Went Out

> Imagine a day when all the electricity stopped working — no lights, no TV, no computers!
> Write a narrative about what happens on that day.

**构思提示**：停电带来什么麻烦？谁帮了忙？有没有意外发现（比如全家点蜡烛讲故事反而很开心）？

---

### 任务 N3：An Animal That Can Talk

> One morning, an animal speaks to you!
> Write a story about what happens.

**构思提示**：什么动物？它说的第一句话是什么（可以直接用它做开头）？它需要你的帮助吗？

---

## Part B — Persuasive 议论文题目（真题风格 prompt）

### 任务 P1：The Best Pet（经典真题风格）

> **What is the best pet?**
> Some people like dogs. Some people like cats. Other people like fish, birds or even lizards!
>
> Write to persuade a reader that one kind of pet is the best.
> - State your opinion clearly.
> - Give reasons for your opinion.
> - Finish with a strong conclusion.

**构思模板：**
- 我的观点（哪种宠物最好）：________
- Reason 1 + 例子：________
- Reason 2 + 例子：________
- Reason 3 + 例子：________
- 我要用的说服技巧（至少勾 2 个）：□ 开头提问 □ 三词排比 □ 强观点词 □ 修辞提问 □ 呼吁读者

---

### 任务 P2：School Uniforms

> Some people think students should wear school uniforms. Others think students should choose their own clothes.
> What do you think? Write to persuade a reader of your opinion.

---

### 任务 P3：Too Much Screen Time?

> Some parents think children spend too much time on screens (TV, iPads, games).
> Do you agree or disagree? Write to persuade a reader of your opinion.

---

## Part C — 片段特训（每个 5–10 分钟，平时用）

1. **Sizzling starts 特训**：给下面每个故事各写一个抓人的第一句（用声音/对话/动作开头）：
   - a) 一个关于暴风雨的故事
   - b) 一个关于丢失的狗的故事
   - c) 一个关于生日惊喜的故事
2. **Show, don't tell 特训**：不用给出的词，把这句话写生动：
   - a) *I was scared.*（不许用 scared）
   - b) *The cake was delicious.*（不许用 delicious）
   - c) *My brother was angry.*（不许用 angry）
3. **说服句特训**：给 "学校午餐应该有冰淇淋" 写出：
   - a) 一个修辞性提问
   - b) 一个三词排比句
   - c) 一个带 definitely/must 的强观点句

## 参考示例（家长引导用，别让孩子照抄）

**N1《The Box》优秀开头示例：**
> *Creeeak!* The old wooden box groaned as I brushed away the dust. It was hiding under Grandpa's stairs, wrapped in a rusty chain, with one word painted on the lid: DANGER.

**P1《The Best Pet》优秀开头示例：**
> Have you ever come home after a terrible day and been greeted by a wagging tail and a happy bark? I strongly believe that dogs are the best pets in the world, and I am going to tell you why.

**批改后跟孩子讨论三件事：**
1. 结构齐不齐（开头/中间/结尾 或 观点/理由/结论）？
2. 找出她写得最好的一句话，大声表扬。
3. 只挑 1–2 个问题改进（比如这次只抓句号大写），下次再抓别的。一次改太多会打击信心。
