# Writing 范文精讲（第二轮扩充）

> 两篇 Year 3 水平的"高分范文"逐段拆解，让 Chloe 看到"好作文长什么样、为什么好"。
> **用法**：先让她自己写同题作文，再对照范文找差距——不要先看范文再写（会照抄）。

---

## 范文一：Narrative《The Box》

> 题目见《练习题-第1辑》任务 N1。

### 范文全文

> **The Box**
>
> *Thump!* Something heavy landed on our doormat. I raced to the front door, but nobody was there — only a small wooden box with my name painted on the lid in silver letters: EVIE.
>
> My hands trembled as I lifted the lid. Inside, on a bed of purple velvet, sat a tiny brass key and a note that said, "Feed me at midnight."
>
> That night, I hid under my blanket with a torch until the clock struck twelve. Then I heard it — a soft scratching sound coming from inside the box. Scratch, scratch, scratch. My heart hammered in my chest. Slowly, I turned the key in the tiny lock.
>
> Out popped a creature no bigger than my thumb, with silver fur and enormous golden eyes. "Toast," it squeaked. "I smell toast."
>
> So that is how I ended up in the kitchen at midnight, making toast for a magical creature called Pip. He has lived in my pocket ever since. Mum thinks I have started eating a lot more toast lately. If only she knew the truth!

### 逐段拆解（为什么这是高分作文）

| 段落 | 技巧 | 评分标准对应 |
|------|------|------------|
| 第1段 | **声音开头** *Thump!*；设置悬念（谁送的？）；具体细节（silver letters） | Audience ✓ 开头抓人 |
| 第2段 | **感官细节**（purple velvet, brass key）；神秘纸条推动情节 | Ideas ✓ 想法有趣 |
| 第3段 | **Show don't tell**：不说 I was scared，说 "My heart hammered in my chest"；拟声词 scratch×3 制造紧张 | Character ✓ Vocabulary ✓ |
| 第4段 | Complication 的高潮：开箱见宝；对话让角色活起来（"Toast," it squeaked.） | Sentence structure ✓ 长短句交替 |
| 第5段 | Resolution + **幽默结尾**（妈妈只知道我多吃了吐司）；呼应日常生活，不烂尾 | Text structure ✓ 完整闭环 |

### 词汇亮点（升级词示范）
trembled（不用 shook）、hammered（不用 beat fast）、enormous（不用 big）、squeaked（不用 said）、raced（不用 ran）

## 范文二：Persuasive《The Best Pet》

> 题目见《练习题-第1辑》任务 P1。

### 范文全文

> **Dogs: The Best Pets in the World**
>
> Have you ever come home after a horrible day and been greeted by a wagging tail, a wet nose and a friend who is always happy to see you? I strongly believe that dogs are the best pets in the world, and I have three powerful reasons.
>
> Firstly, dogs keep you healthy. Dogs need a walk every single day, which means their owners get exercise too. Doctors say children should be active for an hour a day — with a dog, that's easy!
>
> Secondly, dogs keep you safe. A dog's amazing ears can hear sounds long before you can. If a stranger comes to the door, your dog will let you know. Burglars stay away from houses with dogs.
>
> Finally, and most importantly, dogs are true friends. A goldfish cannot play fetch. A lizard will not cuddle you when you are sad. But a dog is loyal, loving and always by your side.
>
> Some people say dogs are too much work. Yes, they need walking and feeding — but everything wonderful takes a little effort!
>
> So if you want a pet that makes you healthier, safer and happier, the answer is clear: get a dog. You will never be lonely again.

### 逐段拆解

| 段落 | 技巧 | 评分标准对应 |
|------|------|------------|
| 开头 | **修辞提问开场** + 画面感（wagging tail, wet nose）+ 亮明观点 "I strongly believe" + 预告三个理由 | Audience ✓ Persuasive devices ✓ |
| 理由1 | **Firstly** 引路；理由+解释+引用权威（Doctors say...） | Cohesion ✓ Ideas ✓ |
| 理由2 | **Secondly**；具体场景（stranger at the door）让理由可信 | Ideas ✓ |
| 理由3 | **Finally, and most importantly** 递进；**对比法**（goldfish/lizard vs dog）；**三词排比** loyal, loving and always by your side | Persuasive devices ✓✓ |
| 反驳段 | 主动提出反方观点再反驳——高分标志！ | Persuasive devices ✓（rebuttal） |
| 结尾 | 三词排比回收全文（healthier, safer, happier）+ 呼吁行动 get a dog | Text structure ✓ 有力收尾 |

### 说服技巧清单（这篇用了几个？）
- [x] 修辞性提问（开头）
- [x] 强观点词（strongly believe, powerful, never）
- [x] 三词排比 ×2
- [x] 引用权威（Doctors say）
- [x] 对比（其他宠物 vs 狗）
- [x] 反驳对立观点
- [x] 呼吁读者行动

## 对照自查表（Chloe 写完作文后用）

**Narrative 自查：**
1. 我的第一句能让人想继续读吗？
2. 我写了人物的感受吗（用身体反应写，不直接说）？
3. 故事有一个"问题"吗？解决了吗？
4. 我用了几个升级词？（目标 ≥ 3 个）
5. 结尾是"然后我醒了"吗？（是的话重写！）

**Persuasive 自查：**
1. 第一段说清我的观点了吗？
2. 我有 2–3 个理由吗？每个理由有解释或例子吗？
3. 我用了 Firstly / Secondly / Finally 吗？
4. 我用了至少 2 个说服技巧吗？
5. 结尾重申观点了吗？
