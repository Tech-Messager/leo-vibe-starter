# Conventions of Language 练习题 · 第2辑（真题风格）

> 40 题：语法 15 + 标点 10 + 拼写 15。比第1辑难度略升：加入名词复数、时态混合、引号进阶。答案在文末。

---

## Part 1 — Grammar 语法（15题）

**Q1.** Which word is a **pronoun**?
*Sophie packed her bag and then she ran to the bus stop.*
- A) Sophie — B) bag — C) she — D) ran

**Q2.** Choose the correct word: *Two ___ were swimming in the pond.*
- A) duck — B) ducks — C) duck's — D) duckes

**Q3.** Choose the correct word: *The ___ are reading in the library.*
- A) child — B) childs — C) children — D) childrens

**Q4.** Choose the correct word: *Tomorrow we ___ our grandparents.*
- A) visit — B) visited — C) will visit — D) was visiting

**Q5.** Choose the correct word: *Ben ___ his bike to school every day.*
- A) ride — B) rides — C) riding — D) ridden

**Q6.** Choose the correct word: *Last week the birds ___ south for the winter.*
- A) fly — B) flys — C) flied — D) flew

**Q7.** Choose the correct word: *Neither of my shoes ___ dry.*
*The puddle was deep and now my sock ___ wet too.*
Which pair fits both gaps?
- A) is / is — B) are / are — C) is / are — D) are / is

**Q8.** Choose the correct word: *Wear a hat ___ you will get sunburnt.*
- A) and — B) or — C) because — D) but

**Q9.** Which sentence uses **because** correctly?
- A) I ate breakfast because I brushed my teeth.
- B) The ground was wet because it had rained.
- C) Because the shop. We went home.
- D) It had rained because the ground was wet.

**Q10.** Choose the correct word: *This puzzle is ___ than that one.*
- A) hard — B) harder — C) hardest — D) more harder

**Q11.** Choose the correct word: *Mia is the ___ runner in the whole school.*
- A) fast — B) faster — C) fastest — D) most fast

**Q12.** Which sentence is a **command**?
- A) Do you like carrots?
- B) Carrots grow underground.
- C) Please wash the carrots.
- D) What crunchy carrots these are!

**Q13.** Which word is an adjective in this sentence?
*The clever magician amazed the excited crowd.*
Choose the answer that lists BOTH adjectives.
- A) clever, crowd — B) clever, excited — C) magician, amazed — D) amazed, excited

**Q14.** Choose the correct word: *Dad cooked dinner for my brother and ___.*
- A) I — B) me — C) she — D) they

**Q15.** Choose the correct order to make a sensible sentence:
*played / the / happily / puppies / garden / in / the*
- A) The puppies played happily in the garden.
- B) The garden played happily in the puppies.
- C) Happily the garden played in puppies the.
- D) The puppies happily the garden played in.

## Part 2 — Punctuation 标点（10题）

**Q16.** Which sentence is written correctly?
- A) chloe and i live in melbourne.
- B) Chloe and I live in Melbourne.
- C) Chloe and i live in Melbourne.
- D) chloe and I live in melbourne.

**Q17.** Where should the question mark go?
*"Can you come to my party (A) on Saturday (B)" asked Ruby (C) with a smile (D)*
- A) after "party"
- B) after "Saturday", inside the speech marks
- C) after "Ruby"
- D) at the very end

**Q18.** Which is the short way of writing **could not**?
- A) could'nt — B) couldnt' — C) couldn't — D) cou'ldnt

**Q19.** Which sentence uses the apostrophe correctly?
- A) The childrens' playground is new. 
- B) The children's playground is new.
- C) The childrens playground is new.
- D) The children playground's is new.

**Q20.** Which sentence is punctuated correctly?
- A) "Hurry up" said Tom, "or we'll miss the train."
- B) "Hurry up," said Tom, "or we'll miss the train."
- C) Hurry up, said Tom, or we'll miss the train.
- D) "Hurry up, said Tom, or we'll miss the train."

**Q21.** My aunt lives in Brisbane ___ Queensland. Which punctuation mark completes the sentence?
- A) . — B) , — C) ? — D) !

**Q22.** Which sentence needs an exclamation mark?
- A) The bus arrives at nine
- B) How do you spell your name
- C) Look out — the branch is falling
- D) I would like a sandwich please

**Q23.** Choose the correct word: *___ time to go home now.*
- A) Its — B) It's — C) Its' — D) Is

**Q24.** Which title is written correctly?
- A) the very hungry caterpillar
- B) The Very Hungry Caterpillar
- C) THE very HUNGRY caterpillar
- D) the Very hungry Caterpillar

**Q25.** Which sentence is correct?
- A) I havent finished my homework.
- B) I have'nt finished my homework.
- C) I haven't finished my homework.
- D) I havn't finished my homework.

## 答案与解析

### Part 1 — Grammar
| 题号 | 答案 | 解析 |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | C | she 代替 Sophie → 代词。 |
| Q2 | B | 复数直接加 s，不加撇号（duck's 是所有格陷阱）。 |
| Q3 | C | child 的复数是不规则的 children。 |
| Q4 | C | Tomorrow → 将来时 will visit。 |
| Q5 | B | every day + 单数主语 → rides。 |
| Q6 | D | fly 不规则过去式 flew。 |
| Q7 | A | Neither 视为单数 → is；my sock 单数 → is。这题偏难，做对说明主谓一致很扎实。 |
| Q8 | B | "戴帽子，**否则**晒伤" → or。 |
| Q9 | B | 因果方向正确：地面湿【因为】下过雨。D 因果颠倒。 |
| Q10 | B | 两者比较 → harder（more harder 是双重比较错误）。 |
| Q11 | C | 全校范围 → 最高级 fastest。 |
| Q12 | C | 祈使句：Please wash...（动词开头，让人做事）。 |
| Q13 | B | clever 修饰 magician，excited 修饰 crowd。 |
| Q14 | B | 介词 for 后用宾格 me。口诀：去掉"my brother and"读一遍——"Dad cooked dinner for me" ✓。 |
| Q15 | A | 语序：主语+动词+副词+地点。 |

### Part 2 — Punctuation
| 题号 | 答案 | 解析 |
|---|---|---|
| Q16 | B | 人名、单独的 I、城市名都要大写。 |
| Q17 | B | 问句问号在引号内："Can you come to my party on Saturday?" asked Ruby. |
| Q18 | C | 撇号替代省略的 o：couldn't。 |
| Q19 | B | children 本身是复数，所有格直接 's：children's。 |
| Q20 | B | 说话被 said Tom 分成两半，两半都在引号内，逗号在第一段引号内。 |
| Q21 | B | 城市与州之间用逗号：Brisbane, Queensland。 |
| Q22 | C | 警告/惊呼 → 感叹号。 |
| Q23 | B | It's = It is。 |
| Q24 | B | 标题主要单词首字母大写。 |
| Q25 | C | have not → haven't（撇号在 n 和 t 之间）。 |

### Part 3 — Spelling
| 题号 | 答案 | 解析 |
|---|---|---|
| Q26 | jumped | 规则过去式 ed（读音像 t 但拼写是 ed）。 |
| Q27 | wolves | f → ves。 |
| Q28 | writing | 去 e 加 ing，只有一个 t。 |
| Q29 | hottest | 短元音+单辅音 → 双写 t。 |
| Q30 | favourite | 澳洲/英式拼法带 u。 |
| Q31 | Wednesday | Wed-nes-day，且星期要大写。 |
| Q32 | heard | hear+d；herd 是"兽群"。 |
| Q33 | everywhere | every+where。 |
| Q34 | disappear | dis+appear，两个 p。 |
| Q35 | together | to-ge-ther。 |
| Q36 | blew | blow 的过去式；blue 是颜色。 |
| Q37 | We're | = We are。 |
| Q38 | its | 它的（无撇号）。 |
| Q39 | won | win 的过去式；one 是数字。 |
| Q40 | see | 看见；sea 是大海。 |
