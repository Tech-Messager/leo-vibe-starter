# Conventions of Language 练习题 · 第3辑（真题风格 · 挑战级）

> 40 题：语法 15 + 标点 10 + 拼写 15。难度对标 Exceeding：加入句子合并、时态一致、直接引语改写、ough/augh 家族拼写。答案在文末。

## Part 1 — Grammar 语法（15题）

**Q1.** Which word in this sentence is a **conjunction**?
*We stayed inside because the wind was too strong.*
- A) stayed — B) inside — C) because — D) strong

**Q2.** Choose the best word to join these sentences:
*The cake looked delicious. It tasted awful.*
- A) so — B) but — C) because — D) and

**Q3.** Choose the correct word: *Each of the students ___ a library card.*
- A) have — B) has — C) having — D) haves

**Q4.** Choose the correct word: *My cousins ___ arriving on Saturday.*
- A) is — B) am — C) are — D) was

**Q5.** Which sentence uses the correct tense throughout?
- A) Yesterday we walked to the shop and buy some bread.
- B) Yesterday we walk to the shop and bought some bread.
- C) Yesterday we walked to the shop and bought some bread.
- D) Yesterday we will walk to the shop and bought some bread.

**Q6.** Choose the correct word: *The kittens hid under the couch when the vacuum ___ on.*
- A) come — B) comes — C) came — D) coming

**Q7.** Which sentence is written correctly?
- A) There is three eggs left in the carton.
- B) There are three eggs left in the carton.
- C) There be three eggs left in the carton.
- D) There am three eggs left in the carton.

**Q8.** Choose the correct word: *Nobody ___ where the keys are.*
- A) know — B) knows — C) knowing — D) known

**Q9.** Which sentence correctly combines these two sentences?
*I wanted to ride my bike. It was raining heavily.*
- A) I wanted to ride my bike, it was raining heavily.
- B) I wanted to ride my bike, but it was raining heavily.
- C) I wanted to ride my bike because it was raining heavily.
- D) I wanted to ride my bike so it was raining heavily.

**Q10.** Choose the correct word: *This is the ___ book on the shelf.*（书架上最厚的一本）
- A) thick — B) thicker — C) thickest — D) most thickest

**Q11.** Choose the correct word: *The team played well, but ___ lost the final.*
- A) it — B) they — C) them — D) their

**Q12.** Which word is a **plural noun** in this sentence?
*The women carried heavy boxes into the hall.*
- A) carried — B) heavy — C) hall — D) women

**Q13.** Choose the correct word: *Please give the message to Ravi and ___.*
- A) I — B) me — C) she — D) they

**Q14.** Which sentence is written correctly?
- A) The girl who won the race is my sister.
- B) The girl what won the race is my sister.
- C) The girl which won the race is my sister.
- D) The girl she won the race is my sister.

**Q15.** Choose the correct word: *If it rains tomorrow, we ___ the picnic.*
- A) cancelled — B) will cancel — C) cancels — D) cancelling

## Part 2 — Punctuation 标点（10题）

**Q16.** Which sentence is written correctly?
- A) in march my family visited uluru in the northern territory.
- B) In March my family visited Uluru in the Northern Territory.
- C) In march my family visited Uluru in the northern territory.
- D) In March My Family Visited Uluru In The Northern Territory.

**Q17.** Which sentence is punctuated correctly?
- A) "Have you fed the chickens yet?" asked Grandma.
- B) "Have you fed the chickens yet." asked Grandma?
- C) "Have you fed the chickens yet?" Asked Grandma.
- D) "Have you fed the chickens yet" asked Grandma?

**Q18.** Which sentence uses the apostrophe correctly?
- A) The three girls' bags were left on the bus. (three girls)
- B) The three girl's bags were left on the bus. (three girls)
- C) The three girls bag's were left on the bus. (three girls)
- D) The three girls bags' were left on the bus. (three girls)

**Q19.** Which sentence is punctuated correctly?
- A) After the storm we cleaned up the branches leaves and rubbish.
- B) After the storm, we cleaned up the branches, leaves and rubbish.
- C) After the storm we cleaned up, the branches leaves, and rubbish.
- D) After, the storm we cleaned up the branches leaves and rubbish.

**Q20.** Which is the correct way to write **will not**?
- A) willn't — B) will'nt — C) won't — D) wont

**Q21.** Choose the correct words: *___ jumper is that? I think ___ Sam's.*
- A) Whose / its — B) Who's / it's — C) Whose / it's — D) Who's / its

**Q22.** Where does the comma belong?
*Before we leave (A) please turn off (B) the lights (C) and lock the door (D)*
- A) after "leave"
- B) after "off"
- C) after "lights"
- D) after "door"

**Q23.** Which sentence is written correctly?
- A) My favourite books are Charlottes Web and matilda.
- B) My favourite books are Charlotte's Web and Matilda.
- C) my favourite books are charlotte's web and Matilda.
- D) My Favourite Books Are Charlotte's Web And Matilda.

**Q24.** Which sentence needs a question mark?
- A) I wonder where my sister went
- B) Tell me where your sister went
- C) Where did your sister go
- D) Your sister went to the shop

**Q25.** Which sentence is correct?
- A) The mens' shed is behind the oval.
- B) The men's shed is behind the oval.
- C) The mens shed is behind the oval.
- D) The man's shed are behind the oval.（指多位男士）

## 答案与解析

### Part 1 — Grammar
| 题号 | 答案 | 解析 |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | C | because 连接两个分句 → 连词。 |
| Q2 | B | 转折关系（看起来好吃但很难吃）→ but。 |
| Q3 | B | **Each of + 复数 → 动词用单数**。这是高阶主谓一致。 |
| Q4 | C | cousins 复数 → are。 |
| Q5 | C | **时态一致题**：Yesterday 全句用过去式（walked + bought）。 |
| Q6 | C | 全句过去时（hid）→ came。 |
| Q7 | B | There are + 复数。 |
| Q8 | B | **Nobody / Everybody / Somebody 都当单数** → knows。 |
| Q9 | B | **句子合并题**：两句是转折关系 → but。A 是"逗号粘连句"（comma splice），语法错误。 |
| Q10 | C | 最高级 thickest。 |
| Q11 | B | team 指球员们 → they（本句 but 后需主格）。 |
| Q12 | D | women 是 woman 的复数（不规则）。boxes 也是复数但选项里没有；D 正确。 |
| Q13 | B | 介词 to 后用宾格 me。去掉 "Ravi and" 检验："give the message to me" ✓。 |
| Q14 | A | 指人用关系代词 **who**（不是 what/which）。 |
| Q15 | B | **条件句**：If + 现在时，主句用 will。 |

### Part 2 — Punctuation
| 题号 | 答案 | 解析 |
|---|---|---|
| Q16 | B | 月份 March、地名 Uluru、地区名 Northern Territory 都要大写；普通词不大写。 |
| Q17 | A | 问句问号在引号内；asked 小写（因为不是新句子开头）。 |
| Q18 | A | **复数所有格**：三个女孩 → girls'（撇号在 s 后）。 |
| Q19 | B | 句首状语后加逗号 + 列举用逗号。 |
| Q20 | C | will not 的缩写是不规则的 **won't**。 |
| Q21 | C | Whose（谁的）+ it's（it is）。这两个陷阱同时出现，是高难题。 |
| Q22 | A | 句首状语从句后加逗号：Before we leave, please... |
| Q23 | B | 书名主要单词大写 + Charlotte's 所有格撇号。 |
| Q24 | C | 只有 C 是真正的疑问句（A、B 是陈述句/祈使句，虽含 where）。 |
| Q25 | B | men 本身是复数，所有格加 's → men's。 |

### 本辑难点总结（给家长）
第3辑新增的四类高阶考点：
1. **不定代词单数**（Q3、Q8）：Each of / Nobody / Everybody + 单数动词
2. **时态一致与句子合并**（Q5、Q9）：一句话里时态要统一；两句合并要选对连词，不能只用逗号
3. **复数所有格**（Q18、Q25）：girls' vs girl's；men's（本身复数直接加's）
4. **ough / augh / aigh 拼写家族**（Q26–29）：enough, through, laughed, straight —— 建议单独做成一张卡片天天读

Q21（Whose/Who's + its/it's 四重陷阱）是全辑最难，做对说明撇号规则已经完全掌握。
