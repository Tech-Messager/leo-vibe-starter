# Paper 1 — Writing（40分钟）

> **Today you are going to write a narrative (story).**

## The Magic Door

One day you notice a door you have never seen before.
Where is it? What does it look like?
What happens when you open it?

Write a story about the magic door.

Think about:
- the characters in your story
- where and when the story happens
- the problem in your story and how it is solved
- an interesting beginning and a satisfying ending

Remember to:
- plan your story before you start
- write in sentences
- pay attention to your spelling and punctuation
- check your story after you finish

## Text A — 故事类

**Grandpa's Garden**

Every Sunday, Lily visited Grandpa and helped in his vegetable garden. Grandpa grew tomatoes, beans, carrots and giant pumpkins.

One Sunday, Lily noticed something strange. There were holes in all the lettuce leaves, and the biggest pumpkin had teeth marks on it!

"Something has been eating our vegetables," said Grandpa, scratching his head.

That night, they hid behind the shed with a torch. They waited and waited. Lily's legs were getting sore when suddenly they heard a rustling sound. Grandpa switched on the torch.

There, in the middle of the garden, sat a fat possum with a piece of lettuce in its paws. It froze and stared at them with big round eyes.

Lily laughed. "He looks like he's been caught stealing cookies!"

Grandpa chuckled too. "Well, we can't be angry at a hungry possum. But maybe we should build a fence."

The next Sunday, Lily and Grandpa built a tall fence around the vegetables. They also left a small bowl of fruit outside the fence — just for the possum.

## Text B — 说明文

**The Great Barrier Reef**

The Great Barrier Reef is the largest coral reef in the world. It lies off the coast of Queensland, Australia, and stretches for more than 2 300 kilometres. It is so big that astronauts can see it from space!

*What is coral?*
Many people think coral is a rock or a plant, but it is actually made by tiny animals called coral polyps. Millions of these tiny creatures build the reef together over thousands of years.

*Who lives on the reef?*
The reef is home to more than 1 500 kinds of fish, as well as sea turtles, dolphins and giant clams. Clownfish, like the one in the movie *Finding Nemo*, hide safely inside sea anemones.

*Why does the reef need our help?*
When the sea gets too warm, coral turns white and can die. This is called coral bleaching. Scientists are working hard to protect the reef so that children today can still visit it when they grow up.

## Text C — 议论文

**Everyone Should Learn to Swim**

Australia is a land of beaches, rivers and pools. That is why I strongly believe every Australian child should learn to swim.

First and most importantly, swimming saves lives. Sadly, drowning is one of the biggest dangers for young children. A child who can swim and float is much safer near water.

Secondly, swimming is fantastic exercise. It makes your heart, lungs and muscles strong — and unlike many sports, it is easy on your joints.

Finally, swimming is fun! Pool parties, beach days and water slides are all better when you can swim with confidence.

Some children say learning to swim is scary. That may be true at first, but good teachers make lessons safe and enjoyable, and the fear soon disappears.

Swimming is a skill for life. Learn it once, and you will use it forever.
